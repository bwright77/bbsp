Roll to the Polls this Election Day

by Odochi Akwani, Writer and Content Manager

November 1, 2024

Shared micromobility like bike share is a great way to get to the polls on November 5 — no one’s right to vote should rely on access to a car. 

There are several ways you can support biking this Election Day! Before heading to the polls, visit PeopleForBikes’ VoteForBikes page to explore pro-bike measures on your ballot that can provide funding to expand and improve safe, fun, and connected places to ride a bike. Then, hop on your bike or shared micromobility and ride to the ballot box. We collected a comprehensive list of shared micromobility systems offering free or reduced-cost rides on Election Day.
